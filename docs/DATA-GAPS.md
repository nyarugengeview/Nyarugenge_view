# Prioritized data gaps

Status is based on source and file checks on 2026-10-23. Names in an ArcGIS service catalog are discovery leads, not proof that relevant data can be downloaded or used.

| Priority | Gap | Evidence/access issue | Recommended next check |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Confirm official boundary vintage and reuse terms | The accessible NISR `village_boundary` layer has IDs and covers Nyarugenge, but its REST metadata omits vintage/license. NISR Hub lists `Village_Boundary_2022`; that layer metadata and query returned HTTP 500 during this run. | Ask NISR GIS for a working download or item metadata, confirm 2022/2023 administrative IDs and redistribution terms, then compare the accessible service to the verified vintage. Do not use GADM boundaries, which are restricted for non-commercial use and redistribution. |
| 2 | Validate supplied facilities and infrastructure before public display | ZIP outputs are geometrically clipped but missing item-level provenance/date/license. Education has direct headmaster-name field. | Obtain the original authoritative URLs, schemas/definitions, capture dates, licenses and update history. Review privacy; remove direct identifiers if allowed and document. Confirm placement/name accuracy with responsible ministries/agencies. |
| 3 | More recent facility records and service capacity | The supplied point layers are not source-attributed, and no verified public Nyarugenge subset was discovered in NISR's accessible REST services. | Check Rwanda ministries' open-data portals and current NISR Hub catalogue for facility inventories, required fields, dates, licenses and stable IDs. |
| 4 | Land use, land cover, building footprints and settlement extent | No verified, licensed Nyarugenge-ready layer was acquired. The supplied archive's GADM boundaries do not answer this gap. | Check NISR Hub's current catalog and official land/environment/urban-planning agencies for downloadable layers or service endpoints; record classification year and definitions. |
| 5 | Sector/cell official population and socio-economic indicators | 2022 census profile provides ten sector counts/residence; there is no cell-level population table or sector-level projection in acquired publications. | Search the NISR census tables for additional published aggregates. Do not distribute district projection totals or infer cell estimates. |
| 6 | Economy, establishments, employment, agriculture, environment and vital statistics | No suitable validated Nyarugenge values/layers were extracted from acquired sources. | Check NISR EICV7 reports, establishment surveys, agricultural surveys, vital statistics reports and Kigali City administrative publications. Respect survey uncertainty, sample design, and confidentiality. |
| 7 | EICV7 Nyarugenge district indicators from dissemination slides | The PPTX is accessible and its text layer can be read, but many maps/charts contain graphics without machine-readable numeric labels. No guessed values were published. | Human-review original slides and related EICV7 district tables/reports; record exact slide/table, population universe, weighted estimate, standard error/confidence interval, and sample limitations before integration. |
| 8 | Review 2008 baseline indicators | The district baseline PDF is machine-readable and French-language, with many tables. Its values have not been normalized or compared with later censuses because definitions, reference periods and geographic comparability need table-by-table review. | Select priority indicators and have a French-speaking reviewer confirm the table labels, units, population universe, and comparability before extraction. |
| 9 | Georeference supplied raster sheet | `15.tif` has no documented geolocation, CRS or control points and could not be safely clipped. | Identify the map sheet's locality, map grid/projection, scale and control points in GIS software, determine rights/date, georeference with residual-error report, then clip and validate. |

## Confirmed vs unresolved

Confirmed: NISR census totals reconcile with sector sums; 2023–2032 workbook has ten district projection totals; NISR has a public queryable village layer with Nyarugenge ID `11`; the supplied vectors were intersected/clipped; the parcel geodatabase is outside the district; GADM reuse is restricted by the included notice.

Unresolved: whether the accessible village layer is exactly the Hub's 2022-vintage item; license/reuse terms for that service and for each user-supplied layer; education personal-data treatment; source vintage and provenance of other shapefiles; georeferencing/location of the raster; and detailed numeric extraction of graphic EICV7 slides and the 2008 baseline tables; licensing and provenance gaps for the supplied GIS archive remain.
